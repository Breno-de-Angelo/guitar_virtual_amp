\section{Datasets}
\label{sec:datasets}

Table~\ref{tab:datasets} lists the datasets with processed guitar audio. They
fall into four groups.

\emph{Effect sets with dry and processed notes.} IDMT-SMT-Audio-Effects has
55,044 two-second notes and chords of two guitars and two basses through ten
software effects and a no-effect class~\cite{stein2010aes}. GUITAR-FX-DIST
re-renders its dry notes through 13 overdrive, distortion and fuzz plugins with
continuous gain and tone settings (about 250\,h)~\cite{comunita2021guitar}.
EGFxSet records every note of a Stratocaster on five pickup positions through
12 hardware pedals at one setting each~\cite{pedroza2022egfxset}. Hinrichs et
al.\ place processed guitar inside mixes with sample-based bass, keyboards and
drums, the only set with effects estimated in a mix~\cite{hinrichs2022convolutional};
its release could not be confirmed. Rossi et al.\ record 400 DI improvisations
on four guitars and apply five effects in 32 chains with fixed or random
parameters and order~\cite{rossi2025automatic}.

\emph{Amplifier renders of DI performances.} EGDB has 118 minutes of DI from
a hexaphonic pickup, rendered through five amplifier simulations~\cite{chen2022egdb}.
EGDB-PG renders the same DI through 256 BIAS FX\,2 head and cabinet
combinations labelled low gain, crunch or high gain (514\,h)~\cite{chen2026toward}.
GOAT pairs 5.9\,h of DI with tablature and renders each item five times
through randomly drawn public NAM captures (about 7,000 profiles of over
1,000 amplifiers), a cabinet impulse response and an optional room
reverb~\cite{loth2025goat}. Guitar-TECHS records DI, a microphone on three
real amplifiers at flat equalisation, and room microphones~\cite{pedroza2025guitartechs};
EGSet12 has 12 performances through a miked amplifier without
DI~\cite{pedroza2024egset12}.

\emph{Device sets with knob labels.} ToneTwist AFx holds dry and processed
pairs from over 40 compressors, overdrives, distortions, fuzzes and
amplifiers, some densely sampled over the knobs~\cite{comunita2025differentiable}.
Amp-Space sampled real devices with servo-driven knobs (about 50\,h real,
500\,h simulated)~\cite{naradowsky2021ampspace}. Open-Amp is not a fixed set
but a generator that renders dry audio through 160 crowd-sourced LSTM
captures on the fly~\cite{wright2025openamp}.

\emph{Real multitracks.} MoisesDB splits its guitar stems into acoustic, clean
electric and distorted electric (240 songs)~\cite{pereira2023moisesdb};
MedleyDB has the same classes and some raw tracks~\cite{bittner2014medleydb};
MUSDB18 keeps guitar inside ``other''~\cite{rafii2017musdb18}. None has
equipment or settings labels, and we found no open dataset that pairs
commercial guitar stems with verified equipment.

\subsection{Captures, impulse responses and licences}

Libraries of captures and impulse responses are the natural source of
amplifier diversity, but their terms matter. TONE3000 forbids automated or
bulk downloading and data mining in its site terms~\cite{tone30002025terms},
and its API terms forbid bulk download, caching or preloading of the catalogue
and require that models are downloaded only when a user asks for
them~\cite{tone30002026apiterms}; neither grants a right to train models on the
catalogue. GOAT's renders through thousands of public captures are therefore a
research resource that a shipped model could not reproduce without
permission. Common free cabinet impulse responses (Kalthallen, OwnHammer)
forbid redistribution and modification~\cite{kalthallenfree,ownhammereula}.
NAM's standard re-amping signal (about three minutes at 48\,kHz) is a fixed
probe for making captures in-house~\cite{atkinsonndnam}. The datasets that
allow commercial use and contain real playing with a DI are EGDB-PG's renders,
Guitar-TECHS, EGFxSet, Rossi et al.'s improvisations and GuitarSet~\cite{xi2018guitarset}.
